# Consent and Call Policy

All demo calls must be wanted, authorized, and lawful.

- Testing is limited to the account holder and people who explicitly agree to participate.
- No unsolicited outbound calls.
- No mass dialing, predictive dialing, telemarketing, advertising, political calling, debt collection, or lead generation.
- No purchased or scraped contact lists.
- The demo identifies itself and its development purpose at the start of the call.
- If a tester withdraws consent, they are removed from testing.
- Call recording is disabled in the initial demo.
- The assistant must not request passwords, OTPs, payment-card information, or authentication secrets.

Before any production expansion, applicable Twilio policies and local telecom rules must be reviewed again.
